# Drink Builder

The cafe site where a customer builds a drink, publishes it, and the board votes. The home page explains that idea, then a how-it-works row (Build your drink, It lands on the board, Barrie votes), then a large showcase of the top 3 drinks, then the builder. My drink is always a visible button in the menu, between Build and The board. If this browser has published a drink, the button opens that page, including the Instagram downloads. If it has not, the page says so. Drinks cannot be edited. There are no accounts. Publishing asks for a name, an email, a drink name, and an optional description. The email is stored with the drink and is not shown on the board or the drink page. Each email can publish one drink. The whole app uses Urban Grind green `#274b3a`, cream `#f3f2ef`, white, Recoleta headlines, a plain sans, and pill buttons. The board has two sections. Most popular is a ranked top 3 by votes, and See all opens the rest, still highest first. New is a visible list of the 10 most recently created drinks. A drink can appear in both.

A person can vote once for every drink. There is no total cap, and a vote cannot be taken back. Each board card shows the built cup and what is in the drink. Cards sit three across on a wide screen and one across on a phone. Instagram downloads stay on the congrats page.

## Run locally

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

The app listens on [http://127.0.0.1:43117](http://127.0.0.1:43117).

Drinks and votes are stored in SQLite at `data/drinks.db`, created the first time the server runs.

## Menu

Bases, milks, sauces, syrups, cold foams, and add-ins live in `src/lib/menu.ts`. Each item has a `name` and an `image`. Image fields are empty until real files are supplied. Nothing is selected until a base and a milk are clicked. Bases are Double espresso, Cold brew, Iced coffee, Chai, and Matcha. Drip coffee is not in the builder; drinks already published with it stay on the board. Milk is one choice, in order: Milk, Cream, Oat, Almond, Protein milk, None. Milk stays locked until a base is chosen. Double espresso, Chai, and Matcha cannot use None, and a publish with None on those bases is rejected. Cold brew and Iced coffee can use None. Switching into a base that cannot use None clears None and any sauce, syrup, and cold foam. Sauces, syrups, and cold foam stay locked until an allowed milk is chosen. Add-ins stay available. Clearing the base or the milk clears any sauce, syrup, and cold foam. Sauces and syrups share one section: one sauce and one syrup, or two syrups, not a sauce plus two syrups. A drink cannot be published until a base and an allowed milk are chosen. None counts as a milk choice only for Cold brew and Iced coffee. The builder preview mounts `public/drink-builder/drink-master.svg` once through `drink-renderer.mjs`, then calls `setState` when a button changes. Iced coffee, toasted marshmallow, and sea salt have no layer. A sauce sets both sauce and drizzle. Milk choices share one photo and stay distinct as milk types. Each cold foam uses its own art. Publishing saves a card-sized PNG of that cup next to the database, named by drink id. The image bytes are not stored in SQLite. Board cards, including most popular and new, the success page, and My drink use that file. The Instagram story (1080×1920) and square (1080×1080) are one deep-green field. The saved cup is a cutout with a soft shadow and space on every side. A small cream line reads BUILD YOUR DRINK · URBAN GRIND, and the drink name is large. No person name, email, ingredients, or button. If a drink has no PNG yet, the server renders one from the saved recipe and then uses that file. The drink page still shows the empty Urban Grind cup. Choosing Double espresso lays the double-espresso fill on top of that cup, the same size and pixel-aligned, with the fill's black treated as transparent. Any other base shows the empty cup alone. On that drink-page picture, milk, sauce, syrup, add-in, and cold foam stay off. An add-in disappears when it is cleared. The drink name shows on the cup card while it is typed. The card does not repeat a sentence of the ingredients. After a drink is published, the congrats page can save an Instagram story and a square. Those pictures show the saved cup photo and the drink name.
